Open the app

No app store, no password, no account.

Start here

Tap Open the app above, choose Supporter, then pick your child’s team. That’s it — you stay signed in all week.

Then add it to your home screen. It opens like a normal app and you’ll actually use it.

What’s on it

  • Every fixture and start time, updated live
  • Which pitch your child is on, with a map of the ground
  • Alerts the moment anything moves
  • Weather for Oakham
  • Emergency contacts — tournament staff and the team coach, one tap
  • Parking, what’s on site, and the Wisteria Hotel supporters’ discount

A family without it will be the last to know when something moves. Over four days, things move.

How the coach reaches your child

Through the app, not WhatsApp. No phone numbers change hands in either direction, there’s no group chat with children in it, and every message comes from a named staff account.

It doesn’t buzz the phone like WhatsApp does — messages appear when the app is opened. Please nudge your child to open it a few times a day, especially first thing and after tea.

The GCS Oakham Challenge

A daily photo challenge with points and leaderboards. The photos aren’t really the aim.

Eight squads arrive on Monday not knowing each other. Meet someone from a team you’ve never played. Get a photo with a player from every other team. Find the leading run scorer. None of it can be done sitting with your own squad.

It gives a shy twelve-year-old a specific, easy reason to walk over and say hello. The conversations are the point.

Does my child need social media?

No. If they’re 13+ with an account they post it themselves. If not, they send you the photo and caption and you post it. Same points either way. And the whole thing is optional.

If you’d rather they weren’t photographed

Tell us and we’ll take care of it. Your child goes on a list only GCS staff see. Every entry is checked against it and nothing featuring them is shared.

No wristband, no badge, no list on a noticeboard, and no other family is told.

They can still play. The kit bag shot, the GCS flag, the trophy, a scorecard, a bucket golf shot — over 100 points without ever being in front of a camera.

Worth telling them directly too: “if anyone asks for a photo for the challenge, just say no thanks.”

What we ask of the players

Not of you — tracking down other families isn’t realistic. The rule sits with the children: before anyone else is in a photo, ask them, and say it’s for the Challenge.

“Can I get a photo with you for the GCS Challenge?”

Because everybody gets asked the same way, a child who says no is doing what any player might do that day. Nobody stands out.

Start here

Tap Open the app above, choose Player, pick your team, then tap your name.

Everyone else will have it. Don’t be the one asking what time you’re playing.

What’s on it

Your fixtures and start times. Which pitch you’re on, with a map. What’s for tea. Where you need to be, all week.

Your coach messages you on it

“Warm-ups moved to 13:45.” “Whites tomorrow.” “Come and see me before you go in.”

It won’t buzz like WhatsApp. The message is waiting when you open the app — so open it a few times a day. First thing, after lunch, after tea.

The GCS Oakham Challenge

A new challenge every day. You earn XP, and there’s a leaderboard for players and one for teams.

485 XP all week 11 challenges 8 badges
  • Find the Flag! — hunt down a GCS flag
  • Make a New Cricket Friend — someone from a team you’ve never played
  • Collect The Set — a photo with a player from every other team. 100 XP, takes all week
  • Meet the Orange Cap — track down the leading run scorer
  • Bucket Golf Hero — your best shot. Out of the rough, off a tree, the more ridiculous the better
  • Champions’ Photo — find the trophy on finals day

How to do one

  1. Take the photo yourself. It’s your tournament.
  2. Post it tagging @globalcricketseries with #GCSOakham2026. Tap Copy caption & tags and paste. No account? Send it to a parent and they’ll post it — same XP.
  3. Paste the link into the app. Most are approved straight away.

Get in early. The daily ones are worth bonus XP for the first hour, and being first to finish one gets you a badge nobody can take off you.

Two rules

Ask first — and say it’s for the Challenge. Everyone at Oakham knows what that means.

“Can I get a photo with you for the GCS Challenge?”

If they say no, fine — find someone else, there are seven other teams. And be kind. Laugh with people, not at them.

The actual point

You’ll be there with seven squads you’ve never met. Most of these make you go and talk to them. That’s on purpose — by Thursday you should know people from every team, and that’s worth more than the XP.

Start here

Tap Open the app above, choose Staff, and enter the password from your email. Then pick your team and tap your own name.

Not listed? Tap I’m not listed and type your name — it appears on roll calls and batting orders, so don’t skip it.

What you get

  • Squad list — medical, dietary, and a parent on one tap. Your squad only
  • Batting orders — set one and it lands on the scorer’s screen automatically. The opposition can’t see it
  • Roll call — tick-list register for meals and matches
  • Messaging — your whole squad, or one player privately

Please message through the app

No numbers change hands. No group chat with children in it. Every message on the record from a named account.

Please don’t set up a squad WhatsApp group for Oakham.

It doesn’t buzz their phone, so anything urgent still needs saying out loud — and tell them on Monday: check the app, that’s where I’ll message you.

Before Monday

  1. Check your squad list and tell us anything wrong.
  2. Read the medical notes. There are a handful that matter. Know them before the first meal, not at it.
  3. Send the parents’ link to your families — this page works for them too.

The challenge — what we need from you

Mention it. A word at breakfast — “anyone done today’s one yet?” — is worth more than anything in the app. Players who start on day one keep going; ones who don’t, never start.

And reinforce the rule. Before anyone else is in a photo they must be asked, and told it’s for the GCS Challenge.

“Can I get a photo with you for the GCS Challenge?”

Say it at Monday’s team meeting and correct the bare version if you hear it. If a player would rather not be in photos, let us know quietly — no need to make anything of it.
